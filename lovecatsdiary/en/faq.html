🚀 Getting Started
How do I start using PurrJournal? ▼

When you first open the app, it guides you to add your first cat:

  1. Tap "Add Cat" and enter the name and basic information
  2. Return to Home and tap the "+" button in the lower-right corner to add records
  3. In Settings, customize the foods, medications, and care items you use often
Tip: Add your cat's regular foods in Settings → Food List first so feeding records are faster.
How do I add multiple cats? ▼

Go to Settings → Manage Cats and tap "+" in the upper-right corner. Each cat has its own profile and records.

Tap the cat avatar at the top of Home to switch cats quickly.

How do I quickly add a record? ▼

Tap the green "+" button in the lower-right corner of Home to open the 9 record types. Pick one, fill in details, and save.

The whole process usually takes under 30 seconds.

📋 Records and Features
How do I set feeding or medication reminders? ▼

Go to Settings, choose the relevant list (Food List, Medication List, or Care Items), open an item, and add one or more reminder times in Reminder Settings.

Each reminder supports:

  • Custom labels, such as "Morning medication"
  • Exact hour and minute
  • Weekday settings, such as weekdays only
  • One-tap enable or disable
When you first set reminders, the app asks for notification permission. Please allow it, otherwise reminders cannot be delivered.
How do I view blood pressure trend charts? ▼

Open Trends from the bottom tab bar to view blood pressure history. Charts only show raw values you recorded:

  • Systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, and heart rate
  • Measurement environment and state
  • No value grading, diagnosis, or treatment advice

If the blood pressure module is hidden, enable it in Settings → Show Blood Pressure Records.

How do I customize care items or checkup fields? ▼

In Settings, open Care Items or Checkup Fields, then tap "+" in the upper-right corner to add a custom item.

Care items can optionally include numeric values; checkup fields can define a field name and unit.

How do I edit or delete an existing record? ▼

Tap a record in today's timeline or the calendar detail page to open the edit screen.

Swipe a record card left to reveal Delete, then confirm.

🔒 Data and Privacy
Where is my data stored? Is it uploaded to the cloud? ▼

All datais stored only on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, app data may be included in your encrypted iCloud backup under your own Apple account. Apple manages that backup; we cannot access it.

How do I back up data or move to a new phone? ▼

Method 1: iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is enabled, choose Restore from iCloud Backup on your new phone and app data may migrate with the backup.

Method 2: Manual export/import

  1. Old phone: Settings → Export Data, then save the file to Files or email it
  2. New phone: after installing PurrJournal, use Settings → Import Data and select the exported file
We recommend exporting backups regularly to avoid data loss if the app is deleted by accident.
Will data remain after deleting the app? ▼

Deleting the app also deletes all locally stored data, and itcannot be restored。

If you have an iCloud backup, reinstalling and restoring from that backup may recover the data.

Before uninstalling, strongly consider using Export Data to save a backup file.

🔧 Troubleshooting
What should I do if reminders do not arrive? ▼

Check the following:

  1. iPhone Settings → Notifications → PurrJournal: confirm notifications are enabled
  2. Confirm the reminder is enabled in the app (green switch)
  3. Check Do Not Disturb or Focus mode
  4. Restart the app and save the reminder settings again

If the issue continues, contact us.

What should I do if the app crashes or will not open? ▼

Try these steps:

  1. Fully close the app from the app switcher, then reopen it
  2. Restart your iPhone
  3. Check the App Store for updates
  4. Confirm iOS is version 17.0 or later

If these steps do not help, email ziyeapp[at]gmail.com and describe the exact steps that caused the crash.

Which devices are supported? ▼

PurrJournal supports iPhone and requires iOS 17.0 or later.

Please refer to the App Store page for the latest availability and pricing.
