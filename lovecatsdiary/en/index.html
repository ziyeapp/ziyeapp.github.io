Cat care timeline

Every meal, med, and moment in one quiet timeline.

PurrLog turns scattered notes about your cat into a clear, scrollable health timeline. Whether you are managing long-term care or simply want a better picture of daily life, every record stays organized on your own device.

App Store Explore features
Core features

9 record types, one complete day.

Built for the small, easy-to-forget moments around cat care.

Feeding

Log multiple foods at once; calories add up automatically.

Medication

Multiple drugs in a single entry, each with its own dose note.

Care

Grooming, nail trims, subcutaneous fluids, and custom care per cat.

Weight & Blood Pressure

Trends turn into charts automatically.

Litter Box & State Notes

Track type, color, consistency, mood, photos, and notes.

Vet Checkups

Define custom fields and review long-term shifts at a glance.

Long view

Trends, reminders, and review views.

Switch between 7 days, 30 days, 6 months, and 1 year. Tap data points for full timestamps and notes.

Flexible reminders

Multiple times per food, medication, or care item. Morning/evening doses, every-other-day care, and weekday toggles are built in.

Multiple cats

Each cat has an independent profile with avatar, birthday, baseline weight, calorie goal, condition name, and diagnosis date.

Review by type

Photo notes, state notes, anomalies, and tracked items are grouped so patterns are easier to review.

Privacy

Fully on-device. No internet. No account. No upload.

Your cats, settings, records, and photos stay on your iPhone. You can export a full backup, share it with family, or move it to a new phone when you choose.
